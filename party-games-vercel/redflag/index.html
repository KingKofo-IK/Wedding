Red flag, green flag

Vote on a dating habit. Then the twist drops, and you vote again. Watch who switches.

Ready?
Draw a card to raise the first flag.
How to play
  1. Read the habit out loud. On three, everyone calls red flag or green flag.
  2. Tap the buttons to log the table's votes.
  3. Reveal the twist and vote again from zero.
  4. Anyone who switched sides explains themselves. Anyone who did not switch defends their stubbornness.
