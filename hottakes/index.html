Hot Takes

Draw a take. Everyone picks a side on three. Whoever is outnumbered has to defend it.

How to play
  1. One person draws a card and reads the take out loud.
  2. Count down from three. Everyone points thumbs up (agree) or thumbs down (disagree) at the same time.
  3. Tap the agree and disagree buttons to log the table's votes.
  4. The outnumbered side gets 30 seconds to change minds. Then vote again.
  5. Anyone who switches sides has to say why. Pass the phone and draw again.
